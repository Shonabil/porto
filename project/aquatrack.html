AquaTrack
Hydration · Smart Reminder · Mobile

Project Overview

AquaTrack is a smart mobile hydration tracking application designed to help users maintain optimal water intake and prevent dehydration. The mobile app features an intuitive interface with personalized daily water goals, timely push notifications, and comprehensive hydration statistics to promote healthy drinking habits on-the-go.

Built as a cross-platform mobile application with user wellness in mind, AquaTrack combines beautiful native UI with practical functionality to make staying hydrated effortless and engaging. The app calculates personalized hydration goals based on user profiles, tracks water consumption throughout the day with quick mobile interactions, and provides insightful analytics to help users understand and improve their hydration patterns wherever they are.

Key Features

A comprehensive mobile hydration solution with features that are smart, personalized, and designed for your health on-the-go.

Water Intake Tracking

Easily log your water consumption with quick-tap buttons for different cup sizes and custom amounts throughout the day.

Personalized Goals

Customized daily hydration targets calculated based on your weight, activity level, and climate conditions.

Smart Reminders

Intelligent push notification system that reminds you to drink water at optimal intervals based on your schedule and habits.

Progress Analytics

Visual charts and statistics showing your hydration trends, streaks, and achievements over days, weeks, and months.

Achievements System

Unlock badges and milestones as you build consistent hydration habits and reach your wellness goals.

Native Mobile UI

Beautiful, mobile-optimized interface with quick tap actions and real-time progress visualization designed for one-handed use.

Development Process

The development process focused on creating a health-focused native mobile application that makes hydration tracking simple and motivating. Research into hydration science, mobile user behavior patterns, and wellness app design informed every decision, ensuring the app effectively supports users in building better drinking habits through their smartphones.

Built using Flutter and Dart for cross-platform mobile development on iOS and Android, the application prioritizes native performance and beautiful mobile UI. Data persistence is handled through SQLite and Shared Preferences, while state management utilizes Provider. Special attention was given to creating engaging visual progress indicators, smart reminder algorithms using local push notifications, and a gamification system that encourages consistent usage without being intrusive.

Technologies Used

Flutter Dart SQLite Shared Preferences Provider Local Notifications
