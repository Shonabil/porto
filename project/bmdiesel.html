BmDiesel
Service Management · Customer Portal · Laravel

Project Overview

BmDiesel is a service management information system for diesel engine repair workshops that transforms manual paper-based record-keeping into a centralized digital platform. Used by admin and workshop staff, it records every service job in full — from unit and engine data, replaced components, estimated and actual costs, to warranty dates — while providing customers with a dedicated portal to monitor the status of their unit in real time.

The main benefits of the system are transparency and operational efficiency. Customers can see service progress in real time through work phases, receive automatic notifications when their service is finished, and make payments by uploading transfer proof that is verified directly by the admin. For the workshop, all data is stored centrally, invoices can be printed and downloaded as PDF, service reports can be exported, and payments are validated with a clear verification flow — reducing recording errors and speeding up service.

Key Features

A reporting platform with features that are modern, structured, and easy to adopt.

Complete Job Service Management

Service recording with more than 30 fields (customer identity, machine/unit specifications, spare parts, estimated & actual costs, PO/receipt numbers, warranty period), complete with edit and delete.

Customer Portal

Customers monitor their service list, progress, bills, and photo documentation of their unit through a secure personal dashboard.

Work Phase Tracking

Progress is visualized in 7 phases (Initial Analysis → Disassembly → Cleaning → Waiting for Parts → Assembly → Testing → Completed) with automatic status (Pending / In Progress / Completed).

Payment & Verification

Payment page with transfer proof upload; admin reviews, approves, or rejects with notes; bill status changes to PAID / PENDING VERIFICATION / UNPAID and the customer gets notified.

Invoice & Reports

Print-ready invoices, PDF downloads (dompdf), service reports with period/status filters, and CSV export.

Notifications & Security

Database notifications with real-time polling for admin (new jobs, payments) and customers (progress, done, payment accepted/rejected), plus role-based access control (admin/user) and data ownership protection (IDOR).

Development Process

The project was developed iteratively using the Laravel 12 framework with an MVC architecture. The first stage was designing the database structure through migrations — covering the users, service_jobs, service_photos, and notifications tables — as a clean, easy-to-develop data foundation. After that, the core features were built step by step: job service management, customer portal, payment flow, then reports and invoices.

The development started from backend design and data structure, then continued with front-end integration using Blade, Tailwind CSS, and Alpine.js compiled through Vite. Each module was functionally tested before adding the next, so the application grows modularly and stays maintainable. The interface is responsive for both desktop and mobile devices, and the application is ready to run in a production environment with a database schema managed through migrations.
